# DLD · Unit 1 — Lesson Plan & Coverage Checklist
**Weightage:** 15 % (~10–11 marks of 70) · **Mode:** EXAM FOCUS (dates TBD) · **Time:** 2 sessions × 2 h + 30 min test

## Learning objectives (CO1 — Remember/Understand)
By the end you can: (1) explain digital systems & gates, (2) draw all 7 gates + truth tables + NAND/NOR realizations,
(3) state & prove Boolean laws / De Morgan, (4) convert between any bases incl. fractions, (5) do binary +, −, ×, ÷,
(6) subtract with 1's/2's/r's complements, (7) represent signed numbers, (8) explain BCD/XS-3/Gray/ASCII and parity/checksum/Hamming.
