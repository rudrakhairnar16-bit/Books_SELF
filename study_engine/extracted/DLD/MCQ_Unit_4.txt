Unit 4 MCQ 
 
1.  The ______ circuits do not use any memory. 
(a) Combinational (b) Sequential (c) Hybrid  (d)Flip Flop 
      Ans. (a) Combinational 
 
2. Which flip-flop has no invalid state? 
a) SR Flip-Flop 
b) D Flip-Flop 
c) JK Flip-Flop 
d) T Flip-Flop 
Ans: c) JK Flip-Flop 
3. In an SR flip-flop, the condition S = 1 and R = 1 is called:  
      a) Set state 
      b) Reset state 
      c) Hold state 
      d) Invalid state 
Ans: d) Invalid state 
4. The characteristic equation of a D flip-flop is:  
       a) Q(n+1) = D 
       b) Q(n+1) = T ⊕ Q(n) 
      c) Q(n+1) = JQ' + K'Q 
      d) Q(n+1) = Q(n) 
Ans: a) Q(n+1) = D 
5. A T flip-flop is obtained by connecting the inputs of which flip-flop together? 
     a) SR 
     b) D 
     c) JK 
     d) Master-Slave 
     Ans: c) JK 
5. Which flip-flop is commonly used in counters?  
          a) D Flip-Flop 
          b) T Flip-Flop 
          c) SR Flip-Flop 
          d) Master-Slave SR 
          Ans: b) T Flip-Flop 
6.  When J = K = 1 in a JK flip-flop, the output will:  
    a) Set 
    b) Reset 
    c) Toggle 

===== PAGE 2 =====
    d) Remain unchanged 
    Ans: c) Toggle 
 
7. How many stable states does a flip-flop have?  
 
  a) 1 
  b) 2 
  c) 3 
  d) 4 
  Ans: b) 2 
 
8. Which flip-flop is known as a delay flip-flop?  
   a) JK 
   b) SR 
   c) D 
   d) T 
   Answer: c) D 
 
9. The race-around condition occurs in:  
   a) SR Flip-Flop 
   b) D Flip-Flop 
   c) JK Flip-Flop 
   d) T Flip-Flop 
   Ans: c) JK Flip-Flop 
 
10.  Race-around condition can be eliminated by using:  
   a) D Flip-Flop 
   b) Master-Slave JK Flip-Flop 
   c) SR Latch 
   d) Encoder 
   Ans: b) Master-Slave JK Flip-Flop 
 
11. The characteristic equation of JK flip-flop is:  
   a) Q(n+1)=D 
   b) Q(n+1)=JQ' + K'Q 
   c) Q(n+1)=T⊕Q 
   d) Q(n+1)=Q 
   Ans. b) Q(n+1)=JQ' + K'Q 
 
12. Flip-flops are used in:  
   a) Combinational circuits only 
   b) Sequential circuits only 
   c) Analog circuits 
   d) Power circuits 
   Ans:(b) Sequential circuits only

   (a) J=0, K=0 
   (b) J=0, K=1 
   (c) J=1, K=0 
  (d) J=1, K=1
