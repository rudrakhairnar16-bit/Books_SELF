Course Outcomes (COs): 
L1 – Remember, L2 – Understand, L3 – Apply, L4 – Analyze, L5 – Evaluate, L6 – Create

No.

Recall and explain number systems, Boolean algebra, logic gates,

Content of the Course 
Module 
Contents/ Topics to be covered 
Hours 
Weight 
age % 
Module -1 
Number Systems and Logic Gates: 
Digital computer and digital systems fundamentals AND, OR,
NOT, NAND, NOR and Exclusive-OR operations, Boolean
algebra, examples of IC gates, number systems-binary, signed
binary, octal hexadecimal number, binary arithmetic,
complements arithmetic, r’s, (r-1)’s complements, error
detecting and correcting codes, addition and subtraction of
unsigned and signed numbers 
 
 
 
06 
 
 
 
15% 
Module -2 
Boolean Function Simplification: 
2,3,4,5 variable K-map representation and simplification of 
logic functions using K-map, SOP and POS minimization of 
logical functions. Don’t care conditions, VEM k-map, NAND 
or NOR implementation 
 
 
08 
 
 
20% 
Module -3 
Combinational Logic Circuits: 
Multiplexer, De-Multiplexer, Design of decoders, encoders,
priority encoder, realization of Boolean functions using
decoders, Design of half adder, full adder, half subtractor, full
subtractor, ripple adders and subtractors, carry look ahead
adder, serial adder, parity checker/generator, code converters,
Q M method of function realization 
 
 
 
06 
 
 
 
15% 
Module -4 
Sequential Logic Circuits: 
Sequential Logic: Introduction, Classification of sequential
circuits, latch and flip-flop, RS- latch using NAND and NOR
Gates, truth tables, RS, JK, D, T Flip-Flops, Triggering of Flip-
Flops, Flip-Flop Excitation Tables, Analysis of Clocked
Sequential Circuits, State Reduction and Assignment Design 
Procedure, Design of Counters, Design with State Equations 
 
08 
 
20% 
Module -5 
Registers, Counters: 
Shift registers, applications of shift registers, serial to parallel
converter, parallel to serial converter, ring counter, sequence
generator, ripple (Asynchronous) counters, Synchronous
Counters, counters design using flip flops, Johnson counter,
applications of counters 
 
06 
 
15% 
Module -6 
A/D and D/A Conversion: 
A/D and D/A Converters Digital to analog converters:
weighted resistor/converter, R-2R Ladder D/A converter,
specifications for D/A converters, parallel comparator A/D
converter, successive approximation A/D converter, counting
A/D converter, dual slope A/D converter, A/D converter using 
voltage to frequency and voltage to time conversion, 
specifications of A/D converter

15%
